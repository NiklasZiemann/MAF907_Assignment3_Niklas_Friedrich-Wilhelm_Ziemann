## Corrections recorded on the way

- **The trimming window (2026-10-13).** The first version set the cut points of every ratio once, over the whole 1971 to 2025 panel and both populations together, and applied them to every exhibit. The paper trimmed its own 1971 to 1998 sample; its cut points were far narrower than pooled ones that include the post-1998 tails (for common equity over assets the lower cut is -4.9 within the paper's years on its population against -125 within 1999 to 2025), so the replication exhibits kept extreme firm-years the paper had removed, and the report of 12 October explained the resulting gaps by the CRSP link and by "a handful of outliers". The measured effect is under F3 and F13: the paper's windows now land within 0.007 of the printed slopes and within 0.005 of the printed Table 5 components. Found by an independent review of the project on 12 and 13 October; the Julia verification of 12 October had already remarked, in its spec assessment, that "many analysts would trim on the final estimation sample", a warning that was read as a note and not acted on. The first version's rule is kept as a sensitivity row.
- **The country screen on the linked sample (2026-10-13).** The screen had been justified by the all-Compustat Table 1 counts alone; what it removes from the linked sample (a fifth of the firm-years after 1998) was not stated, and its effect on the coefficients not measured. Both are now in F1 and in the sensitivity panel of the report's Table 4; the screen is carried as a flag so that the sensitivity rows can be run.
- **The attrition description (2026-10-13).** The Table A note and F4 called 15,584 firm-years "non-positive or missing net assets"; 15,584 is the non-positive count and 4,776 more have net assets missing. The report's section 2 gave the whole shortfall against the paper's regression N to the net-asset rule, while a quarter of it is trimming (F4, F17).
- **The identity residual (2026-10-13).** F14 said the identity "fails in 13,332 format-7 firm-years of 2006 to 2018, probably the excess tax benefit of stock options". The count came from the review of 12 October, which counted 13,332 over all years and called them concentrated in 2006 to 2018, and the cause was a conjecture because the extracts of record do not hold the item. Both are now measured on a fresh pull that adds it (F14): 13,334 misses over all years, 12,803 in 2006 to 2018, and the item explains 98.6 per cent of the latter.
- **Stale numbers (2026-10-13).** F1 "150,244" was a count before the merger screen (149,905); F5 "7,011 (826 in 1971)" came from the diagnostic run (6,999 and 824), and step 8 drops 3,790, not 7,011; F7 "none for fiscal 2025" was wrong (356 fiscal-2025 firm-years have a CRSP December value); F11 rounded 508.39 and 484.07 up to 509 and 485; F12 compared an F-form statistic with a chi-square critical value.
- **The merger screen (2026-10-12).** The first run of record recorded the footnote AB screen as not applicable because the pull script had selected `comp.bank_funda_fncd`, the first table alphabetically, instead of `comp.funda_fncd`. The second pull holds `sale_fn`; 722 rows carry AB across all firms and years, 592 inside the sample window. Applied as an exact match on the sales footnote. *Note added 2026-10-13: the 722 are the rows of the extract, the firms of this pull (the whole table held 769 rows with AB on 12 October 2026); 691 of them fall in fiscal 1971 to 2025, and 592 is what the screen removes from the sample, the rest belonging to firm-years the industry screen has already removed.*
- **Review item 10, declined and then found to be right (2026-10-12).** An `at > 0` screen was added, then removed on the reasoning that "a zero-asset row produces no ratio and leaves every table on its own". That reasoning was wrong: in R a nonzero item divided by zero assets is infinite, not missing, and 1,055 such firm-years pushed the 0.5 per cent cut points of every ratio to total assets far out (the common equity lower cut read -246 instead of -42 under the pooled rule of the time), so the all-Compustat columns of Tables 1 and 2 in the first draft were wrong (1998 common equity 0.023 where the guarded value under that rule was about 0.17). Found by the internal review. The fix keeps the paper's screen (missing assets only, so the sample counts still match the verifiers) and guards the ratio instead: a firm-year with zero assets gets no ratio to total assets. The regression ratios over net assets were guarded from the start.
- **The sample-size anchor (2026-10-12).** The spec and the first version of script 03 used Table 9's N of about 115,000 as the 1971 to 1998 target and stopped the run at 169,174. Table 9's N is below the Table 1 counts summed over the years (about 150,000) and the paper does not say what Table 9 excludes, so it is not the population. The anchor is now the regression N of Table 3 column (4) plus Table 4 column (4), 147,570, and the per-year Table 1 counts. The stop fired for the right reason (foreign filers) with the wrong number.
